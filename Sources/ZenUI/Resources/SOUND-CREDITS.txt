Real small wooden fish from Kohya-san, Japan, struck with its original cloth-covered beater.
The recording was made with an AKG 414 microphone, Fredenstein mic amp and RME Fireface interface.
This identifies the instrument origin; it does not claim on-site temple ambience.
Downloaded the public high-quality MP3 preview on 2026-10-21:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/607/607215_541139-hq.mp3
Converted to 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit PCM; retained the first 0.65 seconds including the strike and decay;
removed trailing silence and applied a 20 ms fade to the near-silent end. No pitch or speed change.
The source asset remains unchanged and is bundled for offline use.
Playback treatment added 2026-10-22: soften high-frequency transients without pitch/speed change;
add a quiet, damped and diffused room-reverb tail (1.8 s total) in memory, with peak headroom.
This is an added acoustic effect, not a recording of a temple interior.
The selected strike interval stays exact; at short intervals, decay overlaps the next cycle
instead of being cut off. Processing is cached; playback remains fully offline.
